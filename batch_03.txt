230	SAVING THE GAME The game provides a convenient place to save after completing each Act.
231	If you wish to stop playing and save the game in any other place, you can take notes and photos of the current state of the game to be able to restore it later.
232	To save the game at the end of an Act, resolve all the following steps: 1.
233	Note down which Act of the Adventure you have completed.
234	2. Save each Character in a Character tray: place all cards, Hotbars and Character boards in trays and cover them with a lids.
235	You do not need to disassemble the Character boards - the lids will hold everything securely in place.
236	3. Return all other tokens and cards to the component trays.
237	4. Place the Story Tracker in the tray and cover it with a lid.
238	Do not remove any marks from the Story Tracker - the lid will hold them securely in place.
239	5. Place all components, trays, and pouches in the box.

271	PART 3: CHARACTERS CHARACTER BOARD
272	KEYWORDS In Lands of Evershade, each Character has multiple keywords that define them.
273	These keywords are often checked by dialogue choices, gameplay options, and various mechanics.
274	The keywords are written in bold italics.
275	Also the names of your profession and race are considered keywords.
276	All the keywords you currently have can be found on: 1.
277	Tokens in the Character space of your Character board.
278	2. Tokens in the Equipment space of your Character board.
279	3. Face up Action and Reaction cards currently on the Hotbar.
280	4. Your Companion card (except in Combat).
281	5. The space your model currently occupies and the Sheet you’re currently playing.
282	Examples: You have found a Strange Egg Item that requires a Light keyword to hatch.
283	You have that keyword and satisfy the requirement in each of the following cases: PART 3: CHARACTERS KEYWORDS AND EXP • You have an equipped Sword of Light Item, since its token has the Light keyword.
284	• You have a Personal Secret token that has the Light keyword.
285	• You stand in a space on the map that has the Light keyword.
286	• You have a Companion who has the Light keyword.
287	You do not have the keyword and do not satisfy the egg’s require- ment if: • You have the Light keyword on an Item currently in your Pouch (you need to equip it first).
288	• The Light keyword is on an Action card currently face down (you need to refresh it first).
289	• The Light keyword is in a space adjacent to your Character model (you have to stand in that space first).
290	Some keyword checks are worded differently on purpose.
291	Instead of checking if you have the keyword, they may check more specific, or broader conditions.
292	Example: An Action on a tactical grid requires a Ranged keyword anywhere within range 1 of you.
293	This means, if another Character with a Ranged keyword is on the adjacent space, you meet the requirements of that Action, even though you do not have the Ranged keyword yourself.
294	ATTRIBUTES (ATT)
295	The following Attributes describe the basic abilities of a Character and define the base number of dice used during Rolls: • Strength – STR • Agility – AGI • Intellect – INT • Charisma – CHA Attributes are marked with appropriate tokens in the Character board and may range from 1 to 6.
296	Attributes may be increased with Advancements.
297	EXPERIENCE AND ADVANCEMENTS During an Adventure, the Characters will gain Experience points (Exp).
298	They represent new knowledge and skills that mark the Character’s prog- ress as an adventurer.
299	Exp is tracked on the right side of the Character board.
