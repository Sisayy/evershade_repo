510	DIVINUM IN ROLLS The Character making the Roll adds a Divinum die indicated by the Divi- num value on their board to the pool of D12 dice.
511	Each Roll that uses a DIV die has its own rules for its use, usually specified in the outcome and usually uses icons as the full value or half of the value rolled.
512	DIV dice do not count toward determining the number of Successes or gaining Fate.
513	Some game effects may allow you to add more DIV dice to the roll.
514	In such cases, the DIV results are added together.
515	SUPPORT Supporting means to roll additional dice to help another Character when they perform a Roll.
516	The following rules apply to Exploration.
517	For other situations see "Supporting in Combat" (p. 23) or "Camp Sheet & Camp Phase" (p. 19).
518	Companions may also aid in Rolls (see "Companions" p. 26).
519	If Characters are in a Party, and one of them is performing a Roll, all other Characters who meet the conditions of at least one modifier may Support them, according to the rules below: • Each Supporting Character rolls only the dice they receive from modifiers.
520	• If a Supporting Character meets more than one modifier condition, they must still choose a single keyword to apply the modifier.
521	• Modifiers with conditions that are not related to keywords can only be applied by the Character who performs the Roll, and never by the Supporting Characters.
522	• Any Successes rolled are added to the performing Character’s Roll.
523	• Any other effects related to the dice values rolled by the Supporting Characters, such as gaining Fate, are resolved by the Supporting Character.

538	All Secret Sheets contain some similarities such as special rules, Sheet Ac- tions, and spaces.
539	They usually also contain the Objective for the Party to complete.
540	A subtype to Secret Sheets are Combat Sheets.
541	They contain grid spaces and are used to resolve Combats (see “Combat” p. 20).

548	SHEET ACTIONS Secret Sheets may contain additional Actions exclusive to the Sheet.
549	During Exploration, the Party resolves a Sheet Action whenever they are instructed to resolve one.
550	During Combat, Sheet Actions may take one of three different forms (see “Combat” p. 20).
551	SPACES A Sheet may contain two types of spaces: grid spaces and large spaces.
552	Grid spaces are square and only large enough to fit one standard Character or Enemy model.
553	They are primarily used in Combat (see “Combat” p.20).
554	Large spaces can be of any shape (including large squares) and have no limit to models or markers you can place in them.
555	Spaces are considered connected if they share a Passable (regular or special) border.
556	Diagonal squares in a square grid are not connected.
557	During the game you might be instructed to place tokens that modify the spaces, such as: Dangerous space token, Empty space token or Blocked space token.

578	BORDERS Both types of spaces share the following types of borders.
579	1. REGULAR BORDER The only function of this border is to separate spaces.
580	Characters, Enemies, and special models may cross this border due to their Actions (for example, Move).
581	They can also be moved through this border by various effects (for example, when Pushed).

590	MARKERS, DANGER AND QUEST MODELS Storybook or Secret Sheet Actions often place, count or refer to markers somewhere in the game area.
591	Those might represent a multitude of different things from civilians trapped in a cave to the collected influence toward your cause.
592	Special rules on the Secret Sheet or the Storybook will explain each time what those universal markers are in each context.
593	Adventures also use Danger and Quest models and place them on the Secret Sheets.
594	The Danger model may represent a group of Enemies roaming the area or hazardous conditions.
595	The Quest model is usually a point of interest – a quest location or objective to achieve.
596	CAMP SHEET & CAMP PHASE Characters rest on a Camp Sheet – a special type of a Secret Sheet with holders for Camp cards.
597	Each slot in the Camp Sheet is marked with a letter (A-H) – Camp cards can only be slotted in holders with corresponding letters.
598	Camp allows the Party to regain HP, reorganize their Equipment, Advance their Characters, and interact with their Companions.
599	Whenever the game asks you to resolve a Camp Phase, place the Camp Sheet in the available space of the Story Tracker, and follow the procedure listed on the Sheet.
