712	TARGETING Enemy Actions will target Party Members, spaces on the Sheet, or other components.
713	If targeting conditions are met by more than one target, players choose the outcome.
714	The most common targeting conditions are: – The Enemy targets the closest Party Member (regardless of cover). or – The Enemy targets the Party Member with the highest number of targeting icons present on Action cards in the Hotbar or on Companion cards.
715	Both the front and back of a card may contain those symbols, but only the currently visible side contributes its icons toward Enemy targeting.

728	ENEMY MOVEMENT Enemies do not have a set movement value.
729	Instead, they move a number of spaces written on the Activation card.
730	When moving, an Enemy follows a set of rules: • Enemies cannot move through Impassable borders.
731	• Enemies do not move through Dangerous spaces if entering them would kill that Enemy.
732	• Enemies cannot move through spaces occupied by Party Members (Characters, Companions, etc).
733	• Enemies can move through spaces occupied by other Enemies, but they cannot end their movement in a space occupied by an Enemy.
734	When an Enemy moves toward something, the Enemy has to end their movement in a space closest to the target.
735	When moving away, the Enemy has to end their movement in a space farthest from the target.
736	Enemies always take the shortest possible path to the target space.
737	ENEMY ATTACKS All Enemy Actions that have the Attack keyword are considered attacks.
738	They may come with different keywords like Melee, Ranged and/or Spell.
739	Those keywords do not have an inherent function, but might be checked by other effects.
740	Each Enemy attack specifies its own range and targets.
741	Characters may use their Defensive Reactions against Enemy attacks .
742	If not specified, Enemies have unlimited range for their Actions and attacks.
743	AREA OF EFFECT ATTACKS Some Enemy Actions are displayed in the form of a graphic diagram.
744	The diagram defines the area of the Action and the effects.
745	These effects affect both Party Members and other Enemies.
746	This means that, for example, a Fireball from an enemy Mage may kill some of his own allies if they are present in the affected spaces.
747	ENEMY HP AND DEFEATING ENEMIES The HP of Enemies is written on their card.
748	Minions always have 1 HP each, and HP for Boss and Elite Enemies is tracked on their HP dials.
749	To defeat an Enemy, you must deal enough damage to reduce their HP to 0.
750	When this happens, remove the model from the Secret Sheet and flip their card.
751	If the Enemy is represented with more than one model, flip its card only after you have removed the last model for that Enemy.
752	When the Enemy card is flipped, discarded or changed during Combat, discard all components from it (markers, Effect tokens, etc.), except the Activation token if this Enemy has already Activated this round.
753	From this moment on, that Enemy does not take part in Combat – it cannot be targeted by any effects.
754	Enemies with their cards flipped may still resolve some effects.
755	If the card states “Do not Activate this Enemy” the card is fully skipped in the Initiative order.
756	Some Enemy cards have a formula that calculates their starting HP based on the number of Characters ( or ).
757	Enemies may heal above their starting HP.
758	Note: In some late game Combats, while playing 3+ Characters, the Enemy HP may exceed 100.
759	To mark it use the 100/200 HP token.
760	CHARACTER ACTIVATION Each time a white token is drawn from the Initiative bag, one of the Characters will Activate.
761	At the beginning of the Activation, they refresh all their Action cards – flip them from the dark side to the light side.
762	Some Action cards may have additional conditions or costs to be refreshed.
763	During the Activation, resolve 2 Actions – you may spend them on Basic Actions, Action cards or Sheet Actions.
764	Some Actions do not count against this limit – they are called “free Actions”.
765	BASIC ACTIONS Each Character has access to Basic Actions – Move and First Aid.
766	They are listed on the Help card, but here is the breakdown of how they work.
767	MOVE When you use a Move Action, move your model up to a number of spaces equal to your MOB value.
768	• You can only move to connected spaces (not diagonally).
769	• You cannot cross Impassable borders.
770	• You cannot move through spaces with Enemy models.
771	• You may go through spaces with Party Members, but cannot end movement on an occupied space.

777	ACTION CARDS Action cards are attacks, tricks, combat maneuvers and spells that a Char- acter has access to.
778	They can only be used during Combat.
779	Action cards have two sides – light when they are available to use and dark when they are unavailable.
780	The dark side may contain passive effects following the Action, refresh conditions and costs, or in rare cases, even another Action that can be made.
781	Action cards are divided into six types: Offensive Actions, Offensive Reac- tions, Defensive Actions, Defensive Reactions, Support Actions and Support Reactions.
782	The type is indicated by an appropriate tag on the left side of the card.
783	Cards have to be stored in slots of a matching type on the Hotbar to be used.
784	After using an Action card, flip it to the other side (unless stated otherwise).
