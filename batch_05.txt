418	PARTY A Party Member is a term that refers to all Characters and all Companions in play.
419	Party Members may form one or several Parties, depending on their location.
420	Party Members are in one Party whenever: • There is no physical representation of the Party or the Characters in play.
421	• They are represented by the Party model.
422	• They are represented by separate Character models, but share the same space on the current Sheet of the Adventure.
423	In Combat, Characters always occupy different spaces.
424	That is why they do not follow Party rules, even though they are still referred to as Party Members.
425	EXPLORATION ENTRIES AND STORYBOOK NAVIGATION A Storybook contains numbered entries for your current Adventure.
426	The Sheets, Actions, rules, and cards of the Adventure often refer to these entries.
427	All entries are sorted in ascending order, though not all entry numbers are used.

431	Whenever you find a redirection with a requirement that you meet (this might be a keyword, Item, mark or anything else), you must follow this redirection immediately – even if only one person in the Party has it – and disregard any other text below the redirection.
432	Example: If you have Dwerg or Warfare, go to 9.
433	If you have Battle Magic, go to 10.
