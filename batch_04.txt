322	MOBILITY (MOB)
323	Mobility defines how quick a Character is and how fast they can run.
324	In Combat, it shows how many spaces the Character may move in a single Action.
325	It is sometimes also checked during Exploration.
326	EQUIPMENT & INVENTORY This is the place on the Character board to store equipped items.
327	It consists of 3 spaces for handheld Items, one for headgear, one for chestpiece, one for footwear and 4 for small Items.
328	Additionally, there is a larger slot to keep Fate and brill.
329	HOTBAR This additional board shows which Action cards the Character has prepared at the moment.
330	Action cards come in 3 different types (Offensive, Defensive and Support), and each card has to occupy a matching slot – one card per slot.
331	Action cards may be granted by class Advancements or currently equipped Items.
332	Additional slots (marked with a lock and a number) are available after reach- ing a set number of Advancements.
333	Example: Offensive cards have to be placed in a slot with a matching type.
334	Some slots may hold different card types.
335	POUCH This is the personal storage of a Character.
336	All Items and Action cards not currently in use, as well as Advancement tokens, are placed in the Pouch.
337	Characters can reequip Items and Action cards from the Pouch during Camp.
338	All keywords on components in the Pouch are not active and cannot be used.
339	However, if the story checks a requirement of an Item, the requirement is met regardless if it is in the Equipment or Pouch.
340	BRILL Brill is the currency used in Lands of Evershade.
341	Each Character has their own brill stored in the inventory space on their Character board.
342	When the Party gains brill, it may be divided between the Characters in any way, unless stated otherwise.
343	When brill has to be spent, the Party may divide the cost however they like, unless stated otherwise.
344	FATE / Fate represents the forces of destiny that shape the story of your Character.
345	You may pay Fate to increase your chances in Rolls and Combat situations.
346	Keep any Fate tokens you have gained in your Brill & Fate slot.
347	Once they are used, they return to the component pool.
348	Fate is the most likely component to hit the component limit of 10.
349	If there is not enough Fate in the component pool, you cannot gain more.
350	USING FATE Fate tokens can be spent in the following ways.
351	You may use those options multiple times, as long as you have enough : • During a Roll, pay 1 to reroll any number of your dice (including DIV dice).
352	• Pay X to refresh an Action card (if the card allows for it, the cost and other details are on the card).
353	• During Character Activation, pay 3 to immediately gain 1 additional Action.

361	ITEMS Characters acquire Items during the game to power up.
362	Most of the Items have their own keywords.
363	Many of them grant a unique Action card and/or a passive modifier to damage ( ) or armor ( ).
364	Each Character starts with a unique Club/Staff Sling double sided Item.
365	Each time you equip it, you may choose the side of the token to be active.
366	USING ITEMS Equipment is the set of Items kept in the slots of the Character board.
367	During Exploration, only the Items in the Equipment slots contribute their keywords to the Character.
368	Some Action cards also require specific Items or keywords in your Equipment – losing an Item may turn some Action cards unusable.
369	If an Action card is used by an Item that’s no longer in your Equipment or Pouch, return it to the box.
370	Some Items can be used as free Actions during Combat (see “Items in Combat” p. 25).

372	GAINING ITEMS When you gain a new Item, you may immediately place it in your Equip- ment.
373	If you do, you may also place its Action card in the appropriate slot of the Hotbar light side up (if the slot is occupied, move the card present there to the Pouch).
374	Before receiving an Item, you are allowed to check the effects of the assigned Action card.
375	Characters do not lose Items, unless a game effect tells them to.

378	HP, TERROR AND DEATH PART 3: CHARACTERS HP In Lands of Evershade, HP represents minor injuries, vigor and stamina during Combat.
379	These points reset at each Camp Phase and in some story moments.
380	Characters may also regain HP with heal effects.
381	Maximum HP is marked on the Character board with the HP limiters.
382	During Exploration, after reaching 0 HP, a Character gains the Injury Condition and 1 Fate, then rolls their DIV die and restores a number of HP equal to the result.
383	In Combat, when a Character drops to 0 HP, they are Knocked Out or Eliminated (see “Combat” p. 20).
384	CONDITIONS Conditions, represented by tokens such as Injury or Trauma, are more serious, lingering afflictions that you have to take care of (for example, in a Camp or by visiting a healer).
385	There are 4 different Conditions: Injury, Trauma, Sunbleached and Dimrot.
386	A Character can never have both Sun- bleached and Dimrot Conditions at once – if they would gain one of them while having the other, simply flip the token to the other side.
387	If a Character would gain a second Sunbleached or Dimrot they gain an Injury instead.
388	If a Character has three Condition tokens and has to gain a fourth, they die (see below).
