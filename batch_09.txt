840	EFFECT TOKENS Some Action cards use Effect tokens to mark ongoing effects that affect the game.
841	Effect tokens can be placed on both Secret Sheet spaces and on Character portraits and Enemy cards participating in Combat.
842	Effect tokens do not block spaces that they occupy.
843	• If the effect says to place an Effect token on a Character, Companion or Enemy, place the token on the appropriate portrait or card.
844	Usually the Action that placed the Effect token will specify how it works in the game.
845	• Each Character has access to 8 Effect tokens in their color with two different icons.
846	• One token has to be placed on a card that is the source of the effect and the other tokens are used to mark what is affected by it.
847	• A Character may have up to 2 effects that use Effect tokens in play at any given moment.
848	If they were to have a third, they may discard all Effect tokens with one icon first to accommodate it.
849	Enemies may also use Effect tokens – they are marked with a square.
850	SHEET ACTIONS DURING COMBAT Some Combat Secret Sheets may include additional tools and Actions for Party Members to use.
851	They come in three different types: • Printed Action cards – Party Members may use those Actions as if they were on their Hotbar.
852	They are not flipped after use, but might be restricted to a single use per Activation.
853	• Additional Action cards – similar to standard Action cards, but placed next to the Secret Sheet.
854	Party Members may use them as if they were on their Hotbars.
855	They are flipped after use.
856	• Non-card Sheet Actions – additional, other Actions.
857	Party Members may spend one of their Actions during Activation to resolve them, similarly to a Basic Action.
858	ITEMS IN COMBAT Item Actions are a special type of Actions that appear only on some Item tokens.
859	Items can be used only in Combat.
860	The access to keywords they provide or to passive effects (see below) is an exception and it can be done also during Exploration.
861	Using an Item Action is free and is not included in the limit of two Actions per Activation.
862	You may use Item Actions during your Activation before or after taking an Action or in a moment specified by the token (even outside of your Activation).
863	When you use an Item Action, discard the token from your Inventory to your Pouch.
864	You can equip it again during the Camp Phase.
865	The effect of the Item Action is always stated on the back of the Item, and the front side stands only for a reminder.

881	ARMOR Both Characters and Enemies may have armor.
882	Whenever damage is dealt to a Party Member or Enemy, reduce that amount by their armor .
883	If there are multiple effects of armor for a single Character or Enemy, they add together.
884	There may also be effects that allow the attacker to ignore the armor of the attacked model.
885	In such a case, temporarily reduce the armor value by the value of the Ignore Armor effect.
886	This may reduce the value down to 0.
887	LOSING HP Some effects in the game may instruct Characters or Enemies to lose a set number of HP instead of dealing – this will be marked as “lose HP” and will disregard armor.
888	DAMAGE TYPES, RESISTANCE AND WEAKNESS During Adventures, Characters may gain access to different weapons and spells.
889	Some of them may deal different types of damage – for example: Divinum, Fire, Ice, Thunder, Light, Darkness, etc.
890	Some Enemies may have Resistance or Weakness to some of the damage types.
891	They are marked on their cards with either “Weakness: …” or “Resistance: …” respectively.
892	Each time this Enemy is dealt damage of this type, apply the corresponding modifier.
893	Weaknesses and Resistances are applied after armor.
894	PART 4: DETAILED RULES COMPANIONS Some Enemies may have a Weakness/Resistance to either melee or ranged attacks.
895	This does not check the type of damage dealt, but rather what kind of weapon was used during this attack.
896	If the Action card has a “requires melee”, its damage is considered damage from a melee attack.
897	PUSH, PULL AND CRASHING During Combat, some effects allow you to manipulate the positioning of both Party Members and Enemies.
898	They come in the form of Push and Pull effects.
899	Push moves the target by the number of spaces away in a line from the source of the effect.
