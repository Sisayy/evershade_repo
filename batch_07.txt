622	PART 4: DETAILED RULES CAMP PHASE
623	COMBAT Each time you encounter a "Start the Combat" instruction in a Storybook entry, resolve the Combat according to the rules below.
624	Some rules, like performing Rolls, are similar to the Exploration part of the Adventure.
625	Combat is played in Rounds, each divided into Activations of Characters and Enemies.
626	The Round ends after all Party Members and Enemies perform their Activation.
627	Over the course of a Round, each Character and each Enemy will become Activated.
628	The order is decided by drawing tokens from the Initiative bag.
629	Activated Characters perform two Actions (see “Character Activation” p. 22).
630	Activated Enemies first resolve the rules on their Enemy card, and then the top Activation card from their Activation deck.
631	After the Activation, draw another token and resolve another Activation to continue the Combat.
632	COMBAT SETUP The setup of each Combat is described in the Storybook.
633	A Combat setup will typically prepare the following components: • Secret Sheet with grid spaces.
634	• Initiative bag and Activation tokens.
635	• Enemy cards that form a row (the card on the left will be Activated first).
636	• Enemy Activation cards that form small face-down decks under some Enemies.
637	COMBAT PART 4: DETAILED RULES • Enemy models.
638	• Character models.
639	ROUND STRUCTURE The Initiative bag determines the order of Activations in Combat.
640	Charac- ters and Enemies Activate one by one according to the drawn tokens.
641	The initial number of Activation tokens is always determined by the Com- bat setup in the Storybook.
642	However, various game effects can add tokens to the bag.
643	To determine whether a Character or an Enemy is Activated next, draw a token from the Initiative bag.
644	Depending on the type of token drawn, resolve the effects below: • White token – The Party decides together which non-activated Character to Activate.
645	Place the token on their Character board and resolve their Activation.
646	• Black token – Place the token on the first Enemy card from the left without an Activation token and resolve its Activation.
647	• Dual token – Count the number of white and black Activation tokens already drawn from the bag.
648	If there are more black tokens, treat this token as white.
649	Otherwise, if there are more white tokens (or there is a tie), treat it as a black token.
650	The Round ends after all Party Members or all Enemies are fully Activated.
651	This means either all Enemies have a black token on their cards or all Char- acters have a white token on their boards.
652	When that happens, resolve all remaining Activations as specified: • If any Enemy cards have not been Activated yet, Activate them, going from left to right.
653	• If any Characters have not been Activated yet, Activate them in the order chosen by players.
654	Then, return all Activation tokens from Characters and Enemies to the Ini- tiative bag.
655	Start the next round.
656	Note: In a rare case, when there are no more tokens in the Initiative bag to draw, resolve all remaining Enemy Activations from left to right and then all Character Activations in a chosen order.
657	VICTORY & DEFEAT Combat continues until the Objective listed on the Sheet is achieved (causing you to resolve the Victory entry) or the Party is Defeated (see below).
658	Achieving Victory instantly ends the Combat, including the currently re- solved Action.
659	The Party is Defeated when all Characters are Knocked Out or Eliminated (in other words, each of the Characters has 0 HP).
660	In such a case, immediately resolve the Defeat entry of your current Sheet.
661	A Partner in solo mode counts as a Character for this purpose, but other Companions do not.
662	KNOCKED OUT & ELIMINATION If a Character is Knocked Out, they cannot further participate in Combat, until they get Stabilized.
663	They gain 1 Fate and 1 Injury Condition.
664	Discard their model from the Secret Sheet along with all Effect tokens of that Character, and other Effect tokens affecting this Character.
665	Place one Effect token of that Character in the space where their model was.
666	Other Characters may target this Effect token with Actions that Stabilize (e.g.
667	First Aid, see “Basic Actions” p. 23).
668	Other than that, this Character is immune to any effects.
669	Some game effects may Eliminate a Character from Combat instead – if that happens, reduce their HP to 0, but they do not gain an Injury unless stated otherwise.
670	They cannot be brought back during this Combat and their Effect token is not placed on the Secret Sheet (or it is discarded if it is already there).
671	After the Combat, the Character who was Knocked Out or Eliminated rolls their DIV and restores a number of HP equal to the result.
672	If the Knocked Out or Elimination happens during a Character’s Action, that Action is interrupted.
